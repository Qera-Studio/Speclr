Verified
A check digit or a cross-record agreement. The app knows the value is internally consistent, not merely well-shaped.
Structural
A regex, an enum, a closed set, a date format. Shape is guaranteed; truth is not.
Bounded
Length, range or presence only. Correct for free text, and the honest label where nothing stronger applies.
00

The shared rules

lib/domain/fields.ts

Five builders, each wrapping a validator in taxIds/. Every schema in the app imports these rather than restating them. All are blank-tolerant unless the calling form passes required, because onboarding saves one step at a time and a half-filled record is a normal state.

emailSchema()

z.email() · WHATWG

  • Trimmed, max 200
  • No hand-written pattern: every one this codebase has tried rejected somebody real

panSchema()

/^[A-Z]{5}[0-9]{4}[A-Z]$/

  • Trimmed, max 10
  • 4th character is the holder type, checked against an allowed set
  • Employee default ['P']; a client passes [] and is cross-checked instead

gstinSchema()

/^\d{2}[A-Z]{5}[0-9]{4}[A-Z][1-9A-Z]Z[0-9A-Z]$/

  • Trimmed, max 20
  • State prefix must be a member of GST_STATES, not a range (01–38 has holes)
  • Mod-36 check character recomputed over the first 14

tanSchema()

/^[A-Z]{4}[0-9]{5}[A-Z]$/

  • Trimmed, max 10
  • No published check digit exists, so structure is the ceiling

cinSchema()

/^[LU][0-9]{5}[A-Z]{2}[0-9]{4}[A-Z]{3}[0-9]{6}$/

  • Trimmed, max 21
  • Ownership triple must be one of 14 MCA codes
  • Year of incorporation between 1857 and now

Cross-record checks

clientTaxCrossErrors()

  • GSTIN state prefix vs the address state
  • GSTIN's embedded PAN vs the PAN on the record
  • PAN holder type vs the entity type
Two hints that never block. cinStateHint compares the CIN's ROC letters to the address state (Qera's own CIN carries UW, which no published list explains). panSurnameMismatch compares a PAN's 5th letter to the surname. Both are true often enough to point at and wrong often enough never to refuse.
No identifier is verified against a registry. There is no GSTIN, PAN or CIN lookup anywhere in the app, deliberately: official access is restricted to entity categories a design studio is not in, and resellers want business KYC and per-call billing. Every tick in the UI claims well-formed, never exists.
01

Keystroke filters

components/form/inputFilters.ts

These run before react-hook-form stores anything, so what is stored is canonical rather than merely what is displayed. CSS text-transform would only make a field look right while holding something else, and these values print verbatim on documents retained for 72 months.

numericField(…)integer
  • Strips everything but digits, on the event
  • inputMode="numeric". Day counts, GST rate, account, PF and UAN numbers
  • The caret does not jump: a rejected keystroke leaves the value unchanged
numericField(…, 'money')money
  • Digits and one decimal point, at most two places, via normalizeRupeeInput
  • Never a minus: no amount on any document here may be negative
upperNoSpace(…)identifiers
  • Upper-cases and removes all whitespace
  • Spaces go because identifiers get copied out of PDFs with them, and a trailing space is invisible in an input and fatal to a format check

Client · Identity

IdentityStep.tsx

Step 1 of 7. The only step that creates the row; every later step is an update.

nameReference name
  • Trimmed, 1–200 characters
  • Lists and pickers only; documents never print it
companyNameLegal name
  • Trimmed, 1–200, required
  • What documents print. An invoice addressed to a pet name is not a valid tax document
entityTypeCombobox
  • One of ENTITY_TYPE_VALUES
  • Supplies the expected PAN holder-type character to the tax step
emailEmailField
  • emailSchema({ required })
phonePhoneFieldE.164
  • Stored as one E.164 string, never a dial-code column plus a number
  • India: /^[6-9]\d{9}$/, exactly 10 digits. Tighter than libphonenumber, because every number typed here is a mobile
  • Elsewhere: isValidPhoneNumber against that country's plan
  • Digits capped live per country inside E.164's 15-digit ceiling
addressParts.line1 .line2AddressFields
  • Trimmed, max 200 each. Line 2 optional
addressParts.pincodeLookup
  • Trimmed, max 20 (other countries vary, so no strict rule)
  • India: /^\d{6}$/ triggers a debounced lookup that fills city and state
  • Refills on change, but never overwrites what a person typed
addressParts.city .state
  • Trimmed, max 120 each
  • Autofilled from the pincode; the state feeds the GSTIN cross-check
addressParts.countryCombobox
  • ISO 3166-1 alpha-2, max 2
  • Decides whether the tax step shows the India branch or the foreign one
addressDerived
  • Composed from the parts server-side, trimmed, 1–500
  • Authoritative for printing. Never composed at render time

Client · Tax

TaxStep.tsx · clientTaxSchema

Validated on blur, not on submit, because this is the step where the app knows more than the operator does. Two branches, decided by the address country.

India

gstRegisteredSwitch
  • Boolean. When on, a GSTIN becomes required
gstinGstinFieldMod-36
  • gstinSchema() plus both cross-record checks
  • The state prefix is the place of supply, so agreeing it with the address here is what makes deriving place of supply per document trustworthy
  • Input capped at 15, upper-cased and unspaced on every keystroke
panPanFieldEntity
  • panSchema({ holder: [] }) for structure
  • Holder type checked against the entity type from step 1: a Private Limited's PAN is a C, an individual's a P
sezCheckbox
  • Boolean. Zero-rates the supply under IGST Act s.16
tdsApplicableSwitch
  • Boolean. When on, section, rate and TAN all become required
tdsSectionCombobox
  • One of 194J, 194C, 194H, 194I, 194Q, Other
tdsRatePercentnumeric
  • Number, 0–100. Digits only at the keystroke
  • Entered rather than derived from the section: it varies with the payee's status and drops for a s.197 certificate
tanTanField
  • tanSchema(). Required when TDS is on, since a deductor must hold one
cinCinField+ hint
  • cinSchema(), blocking
  • ROC state letters compared to the address, non-blocking

Outside India

taxIdTypeCombobox
  • One of 8 codes, defaulted from the address country
taxIduppercasePer type
  • Max 40, then taxIdError by type
  • GB VAT regex + mod-97 · AU ABN 11 digits + mod-89 · US EIN 9 digits + the IRS campus-prefix set
  • AE TRN 15 digits · EU VAT, SG UEN, CA BN structural only
  • Other is free text 1–40, deliberately: a regex invented for a country nobody has billed would block a real invoice
reverseCharge requiresTaxResidencyCertificate requiresW8BenE
  • Booleans. Recorded and printed; nothing computes from them
The bound on the foreign branch. These identifiers are collected, validated, snapshotted and printed as the recipient's registration. No rate, no second tax line, no place-of-supply concept. An overseas client is invoiced as a zero-rated export of services under an LUT, which is India's own rule for one.

Client · Documents

AttachmentsStep.tsx · clientAttachmentSchema

A third party's identity documents, which governs everything here.

kindCombobox
  • One of 9: GST certificate, PAN, incorporation, signed contract, PO, tax form, FIRC, signature, other
fileUploadDropzoneSniffed
  • MIME must be PDF, PNG or JPEG — read from the bytes server-side, never taken from the browser's claim

Client · Delivery & access

AccessStep.tsx · clientAccessSchema
kindCombobox
  • One of 11: brand assets, registrar, DNS, hosting, analytics, Search Console, ad account, social, repository, deployment, other
label location notes
  • Trimmed: 1–200, 1–300, and max 1000. Max 100 rows
These fields record where a credential lives, never the credential. speclr has no secret storage, no envelope encryption and no rotation. A password typed here would sit in plain text in Postgres and in every backup. If a field in this step ever starts holding secrets, that is an incident, not a feature.

  • Trimmed, 1–200 each
  • The name feeds the PAN surname hint

  • emailSchema({ required })

  • Integer paise 0–1013 after conversion. Never a float

  • IFSC_RE, 11 characters, alphanumeric only, upper-cased and capped live
  • Optional, but a malformed IFSC is never a legacy quirk. Money moves against this string
  • A complete IFSC is looked up and fills bank name and branch, without overwriting anything typed

  • Trimmed: max 120, 40, 120, 60. Account number filtered to digits
bank.upiQrDataUrlUpiQrUpload
  • Must start data:image/, size capped

  • Never typed. Claimed server-side as QS-EMP-nnn from the same counters table as document numbers, behind a partial unique index

Document editors

lib/domain/registry.ts

Every document type has two schemas. A draft accepts incomplete content and enforces only structural limits; finalize is strict, because finalizing is irreversible and the row becomes immutable.

Invoice and receipt

issueDate dueDateDatePicker
  • isISODate. Due date derived from the client's payment terms
gstRatePercentnumeric
  • Number, 0–28
placeOfSupplyStateCoderead-onlyDerived
  • /^\d{2}$/, and required at finalize whenever GST applies
  • Derived from the recipient, not typed. Editable only behind an explicit switch
  • An override requires a recorded reason, refused at finalize otherwise. Trimmed, max 300
gstLabel notes
  • Trimmed, max 120 and 2000
lineItems[]
  • Max 50. Description max 300, detail max 300, rate an integer in paise 0–1013
  • Draft: quantity may be 0. Finalize: at least one line, description non-empty, quantity positive
paymentReceipt only
  • Date isISODate (may be blank on a draft), method one of 5, reference max 100

Stipend slip and pay slip

gstRatePercentpinned
  • z.literal(0). Neither slip is consideration for a supply, so a non-zero rate is unrepresentable rather than merely defaulted
employeeIdCombobox
  • Required. The pay slip lists employees, the stipend slip lists interns
stipendPeriodStart stipendPeriodEnd stipendMonth
  • ISO dates, optional. Month a string, max 60, because older slips hold free text
paymentMethod paymentReference deductionsNote
  • Trimmed: max 60, 100, 500
  • The note defaults to empty on a pay slip: the stipend slip's wording is a claim about the employee's tax position
lineItems[] deductions[]
  • Earnings max 10 (stipend) or 20 (pay). Deductions max 20, pay slip only
daysInPeriod daysPaid lopDaysnumeric
  • Integers, 0–31, optional
Two finalize guards on the pay slip. Net pay cannot be negative — no lawful deduction leaves an employee owing wages back — and a pay slip cannot be issued to an intern, refused in the action because the schema cannot see the engagement type.

Letters and contracts

bodyParagraphs[]
  • Max 40 paragraphs of 4000. Finalize: at least one, and none empty
bulletSections[]
  • Max 10 sections, heading max 200, up to 30 items of 1000
contract.parts[]
  • Max 22. Finalize: at least one
contract.blanksCompleteness
  • Key max 80, value trimmed max 500
  • Finalize refuses until every blank the text declares is filled. A blank's existence comes from the text, not from the stored values, so this is a whole-document check rather than a field rule
code name scheduleKeyService editor
  • /^\d{2}$/, trimmed 1–200, and one of build / retainer / setup / audit

Known gaps

unfixed

Found while compiling this. None is urgent; all three are real, and recording them beats rediscovering them.

contacts.*.phoneGap 1
  • Four phone numbers per client (primary, billing, signing, escalation) get max(30) and nothing else, while the client's own phone gets full E.164 validation
  • The fix is PhoneField in ContactsStep, complicated only by the mirrored read-only state
studio.phoneGap 2
  • Trimmed 1–30, no E.164 check, even though it prints on every document
  • Smaller than gap 1: one field, changed rarely, by the person who knows the number
clientInputSchema.gstinGap 3
  • The legacy identity-level GSTIN is still max(20) with no check. The validated one is tax.gstin, and no form renders the legacy field
  • Harmless today, but two columns holding one fact is what produced the place-of-supply bug. It wants deleting rather than validating
